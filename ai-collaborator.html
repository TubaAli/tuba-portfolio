The system — two agents, one source of truth

Two humans, each with a personal agent, around one shared outline repo. Follow the numbers.

1 humans answer, do the research — and talk in the channel or in person when needed 2 humans ask their own agent for details or a simple explanation 3 agents talk to each other on the channel — with all the data: slack, session logs, repos 4 simple / trivial tasks: settled agent-to-agent, right there 5 non-trivial: the agent drafts or raises a PR — and waits for the humans' 👍 6 merge lands only after every 👍
